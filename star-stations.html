Game Systems

A compact arcade loop with resource pressure, upgrade decisions, and escalating combat.

Mouse Flight

The ship follows the pointer while cannons fire forward automatically, keeping movement and positioning at the center of play.

Mining Drones

Hovering crystal clusters launches drones that carry ore back under fire, turning resource collection into a tactical risk.

Merchant Upgrades

Score thresholds call in upgrade offers, including weapon systems, auto-mining improvements, and Guardian Drone support.

Boss Pressure

Miniboss salvage, station gatekeepers, mines, laser drones, tanks, and artillery push the player into faster decisions as the run advances.

Audio & Video

Original music, weapon sounds, commander dialogue, and sidekick video sequences give the canvas game a stronger campaign texture.

Touch Ready

The live build includes mobile sizing and touch-mode handling so the game can be played beyond desktop mouse control.
